04 / STANDARD
UNDER THE FINISH

The shine is
the last step.

A beautiful set should not depend on luck. Lune starts with the work underneath it and the experience around it.

01 / PREP

A clean canvas
changes the finish.

Cuticle work and nail preparation give colour, chrome and structure a surface worth sitting on.

02 / STRUCTURE

Length needs
an architecture.

The base, apex and reinforcement should follow the service and the hand wearing it — not a one-size-fits-all routine.

01

Shape first.

Almond, square or stiletto changes where length, balance and pressure sit on the nail.

02

Build for the service.

Natural nails, tips and structured overlays do not need the same foundation.

03

Then finish.

Gloss, chrome, ombré and sculpted detail sit on top of the work that gives the set its balance.

03 / CLEAN TOOLS

Clean work starts
before polish.

Partner stations are expected to follow Lune hygiene requirements for reusable tools, single-use items and a clean working surface.

04 / THE EXPERIENCE

Different stations.
One expectation.

Lune partners are selected and measured against the same service expectations so the location can change without the standard disappearing.
