04 / STANDARD
UNDER THE FINISH

The part you do not
see matters.

Colour is the finishing touch. The real work is in the preparation, structure and detail that make a set look right—and wear well.

01 / PREP & CONDITION

A good base
changes everything.

Clean cuticle work, balanced length and proper preparation give every colour, chrome and extension a better place to sit.

02 / FIT & FORM

Built around
your hands.

There is no default set. Shape, length and finish should work with your natural nails, your routine and the result you want to carry out into the world.

01

Shape with purpose.

Almond, square, short or stiletto each change balance, pressure and the way your hands read.

02

Structure for the service.

Natural gel, tips and structured overlays need different foundations. The right one is chosen before the art begins.

03

Finish with intention.

Every line, shade and detail should still make sense after the first photo—when it is simply part of your day.

03 / HYGIENE & SETUP

Clean tools.
Clear process.

Lune partners are expected to prepare reusable tools, single-use items and working surfaces properly before the appointment begins.

04 / A SHARED STANDARD

Different artists.
One expectation.

Lune is not one look repeated everywhere. It is a shared way of working: listen first, recommend honestly, respect the appointment and deliver a set that holds up.
