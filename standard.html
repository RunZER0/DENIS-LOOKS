04 / STANDARD
THE LUNE STANDARD

The room can change.
The feeling shouldn’t.

Technique, hygiene, care and service should travel with the Lune name before the first coat ever goes on.

01 / PREP

A clean canvas
changes the finish.

Cuticle work and nail preparation give colour, chrome and structure a surface worth sitting on.

02 / STRUCTURE

Length needs
an architecture.

Primer, apex and reinforcement are chosen around the service so the shape has a proper foundation before the surface gets attention.

01

Shape first.

Almond, square or stiletto changes where length, balance and pressure sit on the nail.

02

Build for the service.

Natural nails, extensions and structured gel do not need the same foundation.

03

Then finish.

Gloss, chrome, ombré and sculpted art sit on top of the work that makes the shape hold together.

03 / HYGIENE

Clean work starts
before polish.

Reusable tools are disinfected between clients and single-use items stay single-use.

04 / EXPERIENCE

Different room.
Same expectation.

Lune partners are held to the same core experience: a considered welcome, a clean setup, clear service and work that matches the direction you chose.
