THE LANGUAGE OF A SET

Shape. Surface. Detail.

Most memorable sets come down to silhouette, finish, and the small detail that changes the whole composition.

01 / SHAPE

Silhouette first.

Almond, square, stiletto or something softer — shape establishes the character before colour arrives.

02 / FINISH

The surface changes everything.

Gloss, chrome, jelly, ombré, texture or a clean nude can make the same shape feel quiet, sharp or expressive.

03 / DETAIL

Small choices carry the set.

A line, raised form, colour accent or empty space can shift the balance without taking over the whole design.

THE LIBRARY

Directions worth saving.

Chrome that catches hard light. Jelly finishes that stay soft. Structured forms, clean French, sculpted detail — different routes to a set with a point of view.
