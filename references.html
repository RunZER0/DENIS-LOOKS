02 / REFERENCES
REFERENCE LIBRARY

Save the feeling,
not the copy.

A reference can give us a shape, a finish, a colour relationship or simply a mood. The point is to leave with a set that still belongs to you.

HOW TO USE THIS PAGE

Take one thing.
Or take three.

You do not need to know the service name. Notice what you are reacting to and bring that into the appointment.

01 / SHAPE

Start with the silhouette.

Almond, square, stiletto or something softer. Shape changes the entire character before colour arrives.

02 / FINISH

Notice the surface.

Gloss, chrome, jelly, ombré, texture or a clean nude. Finish controls how quiet or expressive the set feels.

03 / DETAIL

Keep what matters.

A line, a raised form, a colour accent or the balance between empty space and detail can be enough to build from.

THE LIBRARY
